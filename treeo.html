TREEO: quality for an open-source environmental monitoring platform

Sole QA engineer and lead of the 10-person engineering team, through the open-source release in May 2022.

The problem

TREEO is a GIZ-sponsored environmental monitoring platform made up of a REST service, a web frontend and a mobile app. It had to meet BMZ/GIZ compliance and quality requirements, and the team building it was spread across Germany, the Czech Republic and Uganda. The plan was to release it as open source, so the code had to be ready for public scrutiny.

My role

From January 2021 to December 2023 I was Lead QA Engineer and Engineering Team Lead (Project Lead) at Fairventures Worldwide (Fair Ventures Digital). I led the 10-person cross-functional engineering team across Germany, the Czech Republic and Uganda, owning sprint planning, delivery, cross-site coordination and release go/no-go decisions. At the same time I was the sole QA engineer, owning quality end to end from inception: test strategy, automation, CI/CD quality gates and release sign-off.

Approach

  • I built CI/CD pipelines with automated quality gates, so under-tested code could not reach production.
  • I automated regression testing with Python, PyTest and Selenium, and maintained test coverage across platforms through bi-weekly sprints.
  • I set up end-to-end QA processes, playbooks and standards, documented so they held across time zones.
  • Before the release, I directed security audits of the three repositories.

Trade-offs

  • With one QA engineer covering three platforms, I put regression effort into automation rather than repeated manual passes.
  • The quality gates could hold up a merge until tests passed; the team accepted that in exchange for fewer broken deployments.
  • Leading the team and signing off releases myself meant balancing delivery pressure against my own quality bar.
  • Writing playbooks took time away from testing, but it meant the standards held when I wasn't online.

The open-source release (May 2022)

TREEO was released as open source in May 2022, as three public repositories under the Apache 2.0 licence. I presented the release at Fairventures Worldwide headquarters in Stuttgart. The code is the team's work.
